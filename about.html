Our Story

We provide Quran learning programs online across Qatar for kids and adults with certified male and female teachers. Learn Noorani Qaida, Tajweed, and Tafseer from home with flexible timings.

Qurana Academy was built to support families in Doha, Al Wakrah, Al Rayyan, and across Qatar who want authentic Islamic learning in a safe environment for kids. Our patient teachers follow step-by-step learning so children and adults can grow with confidence through online Quran classes in Qatar.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality online Quran classes in Qatar through one-to-one live sessions, certified tutors, and respectful Islamic guidance for every learner.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted Quran learning partner for parents, adults, and expat Muslims in Qatar by combining strong teaching standards with spiritual care. Families can learn Quran online from the basics and continue to advanced paths like Tafseer ul Quran. Learning the Quran is not just education, it is a spiritual journey.
