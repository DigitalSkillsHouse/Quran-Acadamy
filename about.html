Our Story

Qurana Academy was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to make quality Quran education accessible to every family in Qatar and beyond. What started as a small initiative has grown into a trusted online academy serving thousands of students across Doha, Al Wakrah, Al Khor, Lusail, and 15+ countries worldwide.

We combine traditional Islamic teaching methods with modern technology, ensuring that students receive personalized 1-on-1 attention from certified tutors while learning from the comfort of their homes.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality Quran education through certified tutors and personalized learning experiences, empowering students of all ages to connect with the Holy Quran and strengthen their faith.

Our Vision

To become the leading online Quran academy globally, recognized for excellence in Islamic education, student-centered teaching, and making the beauty of the Quran accessible to every Muslim family.
