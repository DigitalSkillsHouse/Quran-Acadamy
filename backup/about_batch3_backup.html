Who We Are

A Trusted Quran Learning Academy for Families in Qatar

Qurana Academy supports families in Doha, Al Wakrah, Al Rayyan, and across Qatar with structured online Quran education for kids and adults. Our classes are one-to-one, easy to follow, and designed to build confidence step by step.

We focus on Noorani Qaida, Tajweed, recitation, memorization, and basic Tafseer through qualified female teachers. Parents choose us for a safe learning environment, flexible timings, and consistent progress reporting.

Our purpose is simple: make Quran learning clear, meaningful, and accessible from home. Learning the Quran is not just a subject, it is a lifelong connection with the words of Allah.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality online Quran classes in Qatar with one-to-one support and strong Islamic values.

🌟

Our Vision

To be the most trusted Quran learning partner for parents, children, and adults who want to learn Quran online with confidence.
