Frequently Asked Questions

How many trial classes are available?

We offer up to 3 trial classes so that parents and students can experience our teaching style and meet their tutor before committing.

Which online platforms do you use?

Our teachers use Zoom or Google Meet for live one-to-one classes, while WhatsApp is used for schedule coordination and parent updates.

Do female teachers teach boys?

Yes, our female Quran teachers are highly experienced in patiently teaching boys up to the age of 15.

Can adults enroll?

Yes. We welcome women and adult learners of all proficiency levels, from absolute beginners to advanced Tajweed students.

How quickly will someone respond?

We strive to reply to all inquiries on WhatsApp or via our contact form within a few hours during our working hours in Qatar.

Will parents receive progress updates?

Yes, maintaining clear communication is our priority. Parents receive regular updates regarding their child's progress on WhatsApp.
