Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Al-Tajweed ul Quran Academy collects, uses, and protects the information you share when you visit our website or contact us for online Quran classes.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

At Al-Tajweed ul Quran Academy, we ensure that user privacy is respected at all times. The information we request is collected solely to provide high-quality Quran education. We believe that transparency is important, and we are committed to keeping your data protected and clearly communicating how it is handled.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the essential information required to facilitate our educational services. When you enquire or register, we may collect:

  • Parent Name
  • Student Name
  • Student Age
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • WhatsApp Number
  • Country
  • Preferred Course
  • Preferred Class Time
  • Messages submitted through the contact form

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used to deliver our educational services. Specifically, it is used for:

  • Responding to enquiries
  • Scheduling trial classes
  • Arranging class timings
  • Providing online Quran classes
  • Communicating with parents
  • Tracking student progress
  • Improving customer service

4. Children's Privacy

Many students who learn with us are children. To protect their privacy, all personal information should be submitted directly by a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors without parental consent.

5. Online Learning Platforms

To deliver our interactive online classes, we utilize established communication platforms. Our classes are conducted exclusively via Zoom and Google Meet, and we may communicate regarding class scheduling via WhatsApp.

6. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use essential cookies to ensure basic functionality and provide a seamless browsing experience. We also use website analytics to help us understand how visitors interact with our site, enabling us to improve our design and content continually.

7. Data Security

We maintain reasonable technical and administrative safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, please be aware that no internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure, and any information submitted is done so at your own risk.

8. Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only utilize trusted third-party services necessary for our operations, including Google Meet, Zoom, and WhatsApp, as well as Google Analytics to measure website performance.

9. Your Rights

As a visitor and user of our services, you have the right to request corrections to any inaccurate personal data we hold. You may also request the deletion of your information, ask questions regarding how your data is handled, or contact the academy directly for any privacy-related concerns.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may change from time to time to reflect updates in our operations or regulatory requirements. The latest revision date appears at the top of this page, and continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
