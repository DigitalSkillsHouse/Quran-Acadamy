Everything You Need to Know

We provide one-to-one Quran learning online in Qatar for kids and adults with certified female teachers. Learning the Quran is not just education, it is a spiritual journey.

You can start by booking a free trial on our online Quran classes in Qatar page. We then match you with a certified tutor for one-to-one live sessions on Zoom or Skype with flexible timings and a step-by-step plan to learn Quran online.
We use Zoom and Skype for video calls. Both are free to use and work on computers, tablets, and smartphones. We send you a meeting link before each class.
Most children can begin from age 4 with short, engaging lessons. Our patient teachers create a safe environment for kids and use step-by-step learning to build confidence in Quran classes for kids, recitation, and basic Tajweed.
We follow a structured curriculum starting with Noorani Qaida for beginners, then Quran reading with Tajweed, followed by Hifz (memorization) and Tafseer. Each course has clear milestones and progress tracking.
