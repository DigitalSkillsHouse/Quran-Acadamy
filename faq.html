Everything You Need to Know

Find answers to the most common questions about our online Quran classes in Qatar.

Our classes are conducted 1-on-1 via Zoom or Skype. You schedule sessions at your preferred time, connect with your tutor via video call, and learn in real-time. Each session is personalized to your level and goals.
We use Zoom and Skype for video calls. Both are free to use and work on computers, tablets, and smartphones. We send you a meeting link before each class.
We accept students from age 4 and above. For younger children, we recommend shorter sessions and our female tutors who specialize in teaching kids.
We follow a structured curriculum starting with Noorani Qaida for beginners, then Quran reading with Tajweed, followed by Hifz (memorization) and Tafseer. Each course has clear milestones and progress tracking.
Very flexible. You choose your preferred days and times. We offer morning, afternoon, and evening slots to suit students in Qatar and across time zones. Rescheduling is easy with advance notice.
We accept bank transfer, credit/debit cards, and online payment methods. Payment is typically monthly. We send an invoice before each billing cycle.

A smartphone, tablet, or computer with a camera and microphone, plus a stable internet connection. Zoom and Skype work on all major devices and operating systems.
Our tutors teach in English, Arabic, and Urdu. Some tutors also speak other languages. You can specify your preferred language when enrolling.
