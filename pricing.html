Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer trial classes?

Yes. We offer up to 3 trial classes so that parents and students can experience our teaching methodology and meet our female teachers before finalizing a plan.

How are fees decided?

Fees are determined transparently based on the specific course, the frequency of weekly classes, and the session duration. Contact us to discuss the optimal plan for your budget.

Can I choose class timings?

Absolutely. We coordinate closely with families across Qatar to find timings that fit your schedule comfortably.

Which platforms are used?

Our classes are conducted via Zoom or Google Meet. All communication and updates with parents are handled through WhatsApp.

Who teaches the classes?

Your child will be taught by one of our highly qualified female Quran teachers from our growing team of three, all overseen by our founder who holds 15+ years of experience.

Can siblings study together?

We focus on one-to-one classes to ensure the highest quality of attention. However, we can easily accommodate back-to-back schedules for siblings with the same teacher.
