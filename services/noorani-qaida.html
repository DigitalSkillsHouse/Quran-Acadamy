Beginner Course

Master the Foundation of Quran Reading

Noorani Qaida is the essential first step for anyone beginning their Quran learning journey. Our structured online Noorani Qaida course teaches you Arabic alphabets, correct pronunciation (Makharij), vowel marks (Harakat), and the rules of joining letters — all with the guidance of a certified tutor.

Whether you're a child just starting or an adult who wants to learn Quran from scratch, our patient and experienced tutors will guide you through every lesson at your own pace. Students in Doha, Al Wakrah, Al Khor, and Lusail have successfully completed this course with us.

Upon completing Noorani Qaida, you'll be fully prepared to read the Holy Quran with proper Tajweed rules, setting a strong foundation for a lifelong connection with the Book of Allah.
