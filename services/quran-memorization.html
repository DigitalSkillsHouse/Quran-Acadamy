Memorize the Holy Quran with a Structured Hifz Program

Quran memorization (Hifz) is one of the most rewarding spiritual journeys a Muslim can undertake. At Qurana Academy, we offer a structured, proven Hifz program designed to help students of all ages memorize the Holy Quran with correct Tajweed and lasting retention.

Our program combines daily new memorization with systematic revision schedules. Each student receives a personalized plan based on their pace, availability, and goals. Our certified Hifz tutors — many of whom are Hafiz themselves — provide the guidance, motivation, and accountability needed to succeed.

Students across Doha, Al Wakrah, Al Khor, and Lusail benefit from 1-on-1 live sessions, progress tracking, and flexible scheduling. Whether you aim to memorize the entire Quran or specific Surahs, we're here to support your journey every step of the way.
