Understand the Deeper Meaning of the Quran

Quran Tafseer is the scholarly interpretation and exegesis of the Holy Quran. It goes beyond mere recitation to uncover the meanings, context, and wisdom behind each verse. At Qurana Academy, our Tafseer classes are designed for students who want to truly understand what they read.

Our certified tutors — many with advanced degrees in Islamic Studies — guide you through verse-by-verse analysis, drawing from classical and contemporary scholarly works. You'll learn the historical context of revelation (Asbab al-Nuzul), the linguistic nuances of Arabic, and how to apply Quranic guidance to your daily life.

Whether you're in Doha, Al Wakrah, Al Khor, or Lusail, our online Tafseer classes bring deep Islamic scholarship to your screen. Study at your own pace with 1-on-1 sessions tailored to your level and interests.
