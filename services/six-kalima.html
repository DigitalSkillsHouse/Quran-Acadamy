The Six Fundamental Declarations of Islamic Faith

The Six Kalima are the core declarations of Islamic faith that every Muslim should know and understand. They encompass the fundamental beliefs of Islam — from the declaration of faith (Shahada) to the rejection of disbelief, and the affirmation of Allah's greatness and the finality of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him).

At Qurana Academy, we teach the Six Kalima with proper Arabic pronunciation, clear meaning, and memorization techniques that ensure lasting retention. Our patient tutors make this course accessible to new Muslims, children, and anyone wanting to strengthen their Islamic foundation.

Students in Doha, Al Wakrah, Al Khor, and Lusail learn in 1-on-1 sessions from the comfort of home. We explain each Kalima in simple terms, help you pronounce it correctly, and connect it to your daily faith and practice.
