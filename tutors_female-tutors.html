Frequently Asked Questions

How many trial classes are available?

We offer up to 3 trial classes so you or your child can experience our teaching style and meet your tutor before fully enrolling.

Do female teachers teach boys?

Yes, our female Quran teachers are experienced in teaching young boys up to 15 years of age.

Which platforms are used?

All one-to-one live classes are conducted seamlessly over Zoom or Google Meet. We coordinate schedules and updates via WhatsApp.

Can adults join?

Absolutely. We specialize in teaching women of all ages, from absolute beginners to advanced learners seeking Ijazah.

Do you teach beginners?

Yes, we have extremely patient female tutors dedicated to teaching Noorani Qaida and basic Arabic letters to beginners.

Which languages are supported?

Our female tutors can instruct fluently in English, Urdu, and Arabic.

Will parents receive updates?

Yes, we believe in full transparency. Parents receive regular progress updates regarding their child's learning journey directly on WhatsApp.
